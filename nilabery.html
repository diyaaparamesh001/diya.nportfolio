AI makes the designs.
Who makes the decisions?

A website was generated. The design questions were just beginning.

AI gave me a great starting point for the Nilabery Studios website, but I wasn’t entirely convinced by the result. The more I explored the website, the more questions I had about its design.

About the brand

Nilabery Studios is a creative studio working across branding, advertising, and filmmaking. They wanted a website that reflected their maximalist outlook, showcased their best work and services, and drew people in through bold visuals and unexpected storytelling.

The thinking before the prompt

Without enough context, AI can fall back on familiar design patterns, producing websites that feel generic rather than distinctive. I wanted to give Claude a clearer direction by defining the visual identity, typography, content, and personality I had in mind.

What I included in the prompt

  • 01

    Brand personality

    Creative, funky, and maximalist.

  • 02

    Colour palette

    A distinctive palette built around Nila blue, berry pink, yellow, and other vibrant colours.

  • 03

    Typography

    Bricolage Grotesque for headings and Instrument Sans for body text.

    Aa Aa
  • 04

    Content

    The studio’s services, advertisements, films, and other work.

  • 05

    Visual direction

    Playful blueberry illustrations, bold layouts, and lively animations.
